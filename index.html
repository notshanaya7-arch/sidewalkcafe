01 / STORY

SINCE 2022

Not just a café. A second home.

We started SideWalk in 2022 with a simple idea: creating a second home for the people of Kanpur.

What started as an idea became a landmark in Kanpur, and then found a new home in Lucknow and Agra.

Because it takes a village to run a restaurant — and nothing would be possible without our team, our community and the people who walk through our doors.

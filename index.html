01 / STORY

SINCE 2022

Not just a café. A second home.

SideWalk started with one simple idea: creating a second home for the people of Kanpur.

What began in 2022 grew into a neighbourhood landmark and then into new homes in Lucknow and Agra.

It takes a village to run a restaurant. And SideWalk is built around that village — the people, the coffee, the food and the community.
